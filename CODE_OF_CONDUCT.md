# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect different viewpoints, backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks
- Trolling, insulting or deliberately disruptive behavior
- Publishing private information without permission
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the contact method listed in the repository profile. Do not disclose sensitive details publicly.
